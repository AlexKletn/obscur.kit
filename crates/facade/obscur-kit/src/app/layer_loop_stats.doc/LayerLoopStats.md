The three phases of a loop turn, timed apart.

Written for a finding the toolkit's own instrument could not show. `[phase-stats]`
brackets the *runtime's* frame — reconcile, style, layout, compose — and stops
before anything is rasterised, so on a loop that was spending hundreds of
milliseconds inside `present` it reported a healthy 28 ms and sent the reader to
look at `style` (audit 3-D, and it is why 1-D took as long as it did).

The three are kept apart rather than summed because the shape of the problem is
that **one of them is enormous and the others are fractions**. A total hides
exactly that, which is also why `perf::FrameStats` — which holds two numbers —
is not what this uses.

Off unless `OBSCUR_FRAME_STATS` is set: a panel printing sixty lines a second
unasked would be worse than no instrument.
